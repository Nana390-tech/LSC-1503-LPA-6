HCT LSC 1503 – LPA 6

Reflection: My Interview Assessment

Before you start

You have now completed your interview assessment. This reflection has three parts: before the interview, during the interview, and after the interview.

Answer the questions below in complete sentences. Be honest about your experience — there are no right or wrong answers here, and your answers do not change your interview grade. Aim for three to four sentences per question.

Part 1: Before the interview

How you planned and got ready
1 How did you plan and get ready for your interview? Describe what you did and when.

Think about: your scripts, the apps you used, practising with a partner or your family, the mock interview, and what you did the day before.

0 words
To get ready, I first… In the last week, I practised… The day before my interview, I…
2 Was your preparation enough? What helped you the most, and what do you wish you had done more of?

Think about: which activity made the biggest difference, and what you ran out of time to do.

0 words
The most useful preparation for me was… because… I wish I had spent more time on… My preparation was / was not enough because…

Part 2: During the interview

How the interview went on the day
3 How did your interview go? Which question did you answer best, and which one was the most difficult? Why?

Think about: your answers, the vocabulary you used, whether you forgot anything, and any question you did not expect.

0 words
My best answer was the question about… The most difficult question was… because… When I did not know what to say, I…
4 What did you notice about your speaking and your body language during the interview?

Think about: your voice, your speed, your pauses, your eye contact, your hands, and how you handled your nerves.

0 words
During the interview, I noticed that my voice… I think my eye contact was… When I felt nervous, I…

Part 3: After the interview

What you would change and what comes next
5 If you did the same interview again tomorrow, what would you do differently?

Think about: your preparation, your answers, and your body language. Give one or two specific changes.

0 words
If I did the interview again, I would… Next time I would prepare… I would not… again, because…
6 What will you work on next? Name one speaking skill you want to improve and explain how you will practise it.

Think about: one clear skill, and a real plan — where, when and how often you will practise it.

0 words
The skill I want to improve is… I will practise this by… I will do this… times every week.
